Meet Coach DK

Eighteen years of making organisations work properly

My passion for business goes back a long way. Long before I called myself a coach, I was the person people came to with an idea they believed in, wanting to know what to do with it next. I spent eighteen years inside organisations, in records, transport, facilities, library services and company secretarial work, learning how businesses actually hold together behind the scenes.

The longer I did that work, the clearer one observation became. There is a gap between the moment somebody starts a business and the moment that business has real structure underneath it. Most people never cross it. They stay busy, they stay trading, but the business never becomes something organised and genuinely profitable. Closing that gap is my mission.

Starting a business is one thing. Giving it the structure to grow and make money is another.

So the work I do is practical. I help people build structure and record the things that matter, so they can see what is happening in their own business, spot problems early and fix them while they are still small. You cannot manage what you never wrote down.

Compliance and registration are a big part of that. Being properly registered and up to date is not paperwork for its own sake. It is what allows you to apply for grants, access government incentives, qualify for funding and take part in bigger trade and tender opportunities. Without it, doors stay closed no matter how good the business is.

My mission

To make sure no good business idea dies of bad structure.
